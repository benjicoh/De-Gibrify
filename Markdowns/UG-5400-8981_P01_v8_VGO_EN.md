# Overview

https://bwiildita.sharepoint.com/sites/prod/Topics/VIZIGO\_overview\_EN.xml

The **CARTO VIZIGO™ Software** allows visualization of the CARTO VIZIGO™ Bi-Directional Guiding Sheath in the CARTO™ 3 System when paired with a sensor-based focal ablation catheter.[[1]](#footnote-1) Sheath visualization aids in understanding the spatial relationship between the catheter and the sheath during navigation in the cardiac chambers.

![VIZIGO_overview.png](data:image/png;base64...)

Sheath visualization is made possible by four electrodes located along the sheath curve. Sheath electrodes cannot be used for point acquisition, signal display, or pacing.

An enhanced visualization mode, *paired visualization*, is available when a sensor-based focal ablation catheter with 6 electrodes is inserted in the sheath. This paired mode aligns the sensor-based visualization of the catheter with the ACL-based visualization of the sheath.[[2]](#footnote-2)

Only one CARTO VIZIGO™ Sheath can be visualized in a Study.

## Building the Matrix

https://bwiildita.sharepoint.com/sites/prod/Topics/VIZIGO\_vis\_acl\_EN.xml

Sheath visualization in the CARTO™ 3 System is based on Advanced Catheter Location (ACL) technology. This technology relies on a combination of magnetic field measurements and current measurements. The magnetic location data and current location data are calibrated to form the matrix when a sensor-based navigation catheter is moved through the chamber of interest.

The better the matrix, the better the visualization. To view the matrix, click Help > Show Visualization Matrix.

![vizigo_matrix.png](data:image/png;base64...)

Figure 1: CARTO VIZIGO™ Sheath Visualization with Matrix

The sheath is not sensor based, so it cannot be used for building the matrix. If a sensor-based catheter is within the sheath, it must be extended outside the sheath before it can be used for building the matrix.

## Paired Visualization

https://bwiildita.sharepoint.com/sites/prod/Topics/VIZIGO\_pairing\_EN.xml

DOR chart, 4.1

When the system detects that a CARTO VIZIGO™ Sheath and a supported sensor-based focal ablation catheter are both connected, the System automatically pairs them. The paired sheath and catheter are visualized as one unit using a unified mechanical model.

DOR chart, 5.1, 5.2

If the paired catheter is disconnected, the sheath is unpaired. When the catheter is reconnected, pairing is restored automatically.

The system cannot detect which catheter is inserted in the sheath. This means that you must manually pair or unpair the sheath in certain situations.

DOR chart, 4.2, 7.2

* If a catheter supported for paired visualization is connected to the PIU while a diagnostic catheter is inserted in the sheath, you must unpair the sheath. After the sheath is unpaired, both catheters are visualized.

If you do not manually unpair the sheath, the System attempts to visualize the sheath paired with the ablation catheter.

DOR chart, 4.2.1

* If a supported catheter is inserted in the sheath after it was manually unpaired, you must pair it again.

By default, both the catheter and the sheath are displayed when they are paired. You can change the display settings.

## Interpolation Limit

https://bwiildita.sharepoint.com/sites/prod/Topics/VIZIGO\_limit\_EN.xml

DOR chart, 5.2

The mechanical model for visualization interpolates the position of the catheter shaft when the catheter extends beyond the sheath. If the catheter extends too far out of the sheath, visualization of the connecting shaft between the sheath and the catheter disappears. Visualization of the catheter and the sheath are maintained.

![vizigo_break_v7p3.png](data:image/png;base64...)

# Safety Information

https://bwiildita.sharepoint.com/sites/prod/Topics/VIZIGO\_safety\_EN.xml

All safety considerations, cautions, and warnings that apply to the general use of the CARTO™ 3 System also apply while using this software. In addition, the following considerations apply:

* Read the *Instructions for Use* (IFU) for the sheath and the catheter before using them with the system.
* Use your common clinical practice for real-time verification of catheter locations throughout the procedure (inspection of IC signals, direct imaging guidance such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, etc.). Failure to do so might result in incorrect placement of the catheter.
* Before applying RF energy, confirm with real-time imaging that the tip of the ablation catheter is out of the sheath.

# Setup

https://bwiildita.sharepoint.com/sites/prod/Topics/VIZIGO\_setup\_EN.xml

Standard CARTO™ 3 System connector cables (yellow or blue) are used to connect the CARTO VIZIGO™ Sheath to the PIU.

1. Connect the sheath to the connector cable before connecting the cable to the PIU.

Connecting the cable to the PIU first may lead to problems with the Auto-ID recognition.

1. Connect the cable to the appropriate socket (blue or yellow).

To ensure best sheath visualization, connect the connector cable attached to the CARTO VIZIGO™ sheath to one of the compatible PIU sockets based on the following order of recommendation:

1. REF/DECA, 20 POLE A, or 20 POLE B
2. DECA
3. QUAD B

![VIZIGO_PIU_connections.png](data:image/png;base64...)

Figure 3: PIU Connections

A change in the connection status of the sheath is displayed in the Catheter Setup screen and in the Main Map Viewer within 10 seconds of making the change.

https://bwiildita.sharepoint.com/sites/prod/Topics/VIZIGO\_vis\_define\_v7\_EN.xml

When you connect a CARTO VIZIGO™ Bi-Directional Guiding Sheath, it is automatically identified by the system. The sheath icon appears in the Catheter Setup screen at the socket to which it is connected.

![VIZIGO_cath_icon_setup.png](data:image/png;base64...)

Figure 4: Sheath Icon in Catheter Setup Screen

Click the sheath icon to display its visualization settings. Adjust the visualization settings as required.

![cath_setup_vizigo.png](data:image/png;base64...)

Figure 5: Sheath Visualization Options in Catheter Setup Screen

Table 1: Sheath Visualization Options

| Option | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Sheath Image | When selected, the sheath image is displayed in the Main Map Viewer and Additional Map Viewer.  If the sheath and catheter are paired, this setting interacts with the catheter's Tip Only setting as follows:   * Tip Only not selected: display of sheath, catheter and connecting shaft   ![VIZIGO_view_full.png](data:image/png;base64...)   * Tip Only selected: display of sheath and catheter, but no connecting shaft   The tip and the sheath are still aligned.  ![VIZIGO_view_tip.png](data:image/png;base64...) |
| Label | When selected, the label is displayed at the distal end of the sheath in the Main Map Viewer and Additional Map Viewer.  ![VIZIGO_view_label_v7p3.png](data:image/png;base64...)  The default label is the connector label in the Catheter Setup screen. In the text box, you can enter a new label for the sheath (up to 3 characters).  The Label check box is available only when Sheath Image is selected. The text box is always available. |
| Shaft Color | Sets the color for the sheath. |
| Paired with | Shows whether the sheath is paired with a catheter for visualization.  By default, the sheath is paired with the catheter connected to the MAP socket if that catheter is supported for paired visualization.  If the paired catheter is disconnected, Paired with is automatically set to None.  If a catheter that is not supported for paired visualization is in the sheath, you must manually set Paired with to None. |

The new settings are applied immediately.

All the sheath visualization settings except for Paired with are saved in the template.

# Typical Workflow

https://bwiildita.sharepoint.com/sites/prod/Topics/VIZIGO\_workflow\_EN.xml

1. Prepare the sheath according to the sheath's IFU.
2. Access vasculature using your common clinical practice.
3. Connect the sheath to the cable, and then the cable to the PIU.
4. Connect the supported ablation catheter to the MAP socket.
5. Select sheath visualization settings, if needed.
6. Insert compatible catheter through sheath.
7. Build the matrix with a sensor-based catheter (therapeutic or diagnostic) to enable sheath visualization.

The sheath is visualized as soon as the matrix is built.

## Pairing or Unpairing the Sheath

https://bwiildita.sharepoint.com/sites/prod/Topics/VIZIGO\_unpairing\_EN.xml

When the system detects that a CARTO VIZIGO™ Sheath and a supported sensor-based focal ablation catheter are both connected, the System automatically pairs them.

If that catheter is not the catheter in the CARTO VIZIGO™ Sheath, the sheath and catheter must be unpaired (Paired with set to None).

In some situations, such as reconnecting a supported catheter after manually unpairing it, pairing must be set manually (Paired with set to MAP).

Table 4: Options for Pairing or Unpairing

| Option | Example |
| --- | --- |
| In the Catheter Setup Screen, set the Paired with option. | ![cath_setup_vizigo_small.png](data:image/png;base64...) |
| Right-click the ![setup_cath_btn_v7.png](data:image/png;base64...) button. Select the sheath and set the Paired with option. | ![VIZIGO_cath_list_v7.png](data:image/png;base64...) |
| Right-click the sheath visualization and set the Paired with option. | ![vizigo_sheath_none_v7p3.png](data:image/png;base64...) |

The sheath can be paired or unpaired through the same options.

## Troubleshooting CARTO VIZIGO™ Sheath Visualization

https://bwiildita.sharepoint.com/sites/prod/Topics/VIZIGO\_troubleshooting\_EN.xml

Training

| Problem | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| Sheath is not visualized | Try the following actions until the issue is resolved:   * Ensure that Sheath Image is checked in the Catheter Setup Screen. * Click Help > Show Visualization Matrix. If the matrix is incomplete, add more data. (Rotate the map viewer to view the matrix in other orientations.) |
| Stripes appear on catheter or sheath | Stripes indicate reduced accuracy.  Try the following actions until the issue is resolved:   * Add matrix data.   If the sheath is partially in the right atrium (RA), add matrix data in the RA near the septum.   * Check for patch movement. * Check for magnetic interference. |
| Break between sheath and catheter visualization when catheter tip is far from sheath | This is standard system behavior. The break disappears when the catheter is drawn back into the sheath.  DOR chart, 8  See ["Interpolation Limit"](#target9000004). |
| Break between sheath and catheter visualization, but catheter is close to sheath | Try the following actions until the issue is resolved:   * Add matrix data. * Reset sheath pairing: click Catheter > Reset Sheath Pairing |
| Catheter and sheath are not immediately visualized after reconnecting catheter | If the shaft between sheath and catheter tip was in a complex curve (for example, S-curve or loop) when the catheter was disconnected, Both the catheter and the sheath visualization disappear. They are unpaired.  When the catheter is reconnected, there may be a long delay before visualization is restored. When it is restored, pairing and sheath visualization are reset as expected.  DOR chart, 10, 10.1 |
| Appearance of shaft between sheath and catheter tip is distorted or not logical | * If catheter in sheath is supported for paired visualization, reset sheath pairing. Click Catheter > Reset Sheath Pairing * If catheter in the sheath is not supported for paired visualization, ensure that the sheath is unpaired.   See ["Pairing or Unpairing the Sheath"](#target9000005). |
